# Decision: plain, short copy across the site

**Date:** 2026-11-23
**Decided by:** Andria ("Yes to all" on ten questions, `docs/2026-11-23-writing-guardrails-questions.md`)

## Context

Andria reviewed the site on her phone and flagged four examples: a card ending "fully
sourced and credited", a stat box with a third line about "charter LEAs", a three-line
bill tracker description, and the button "Seen the claims? Now see the record". Her
words: "I really hate finding the duplicative or overexplanatory language and the AI
tells throughout."

A read of every writing rule found that they covered casing, em-dashes, emoji, naming and
sourcing, and nothing on length, reassurance words, jargon, or what a card, a button or a
stat box should say. A scan of the main site found 172 places to simplify.

## Decision

The ten rules now in `DESIGN-GUARDRAILS.md` under "Plain, short copy", plus two readings:

- Sourcing stays, silently. Every claim keeps its source in the Sources list. The page
  does not say that it is sourced.
- A later instruction from Andria replaces an earlier approved line.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Keep question openers** (the July strategy note asked for "questions posed"). Rejected:
  they read as slogans.
- **Keep third lines on stat boxes for precision.** Rejected: too much detail for a general
  reader. The detail moves to Sources.
- **Exempt talking points** because they are meant to be spoken. Rejected: party labels,
  emoji and slogans there contradict the rest of the site.

## Scope

representdc-main. The bill tracker and the candidates site have no writing file and were
not scanned; they should adopt these rules when next edited.
