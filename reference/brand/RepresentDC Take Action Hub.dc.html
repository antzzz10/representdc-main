Latest statehood news

A quick skim of recent developments — full detail and vote history live in the Bill Tracker.

DEC 2025

Rep. Nancy Mace introduces a bill to redesignate Black Lives Matter Plaza — a locally-chosen name — as "Charlie Kirk Freedom of Speech Plaza."

Source: Anti-DC Bill Tracker

MAR 2025

A House continuing resolution omits the routine language letting D.C. spend its own already-approved budget — blocking roughly $1.1 billion in local funds.

Source: Anti-DC Bill Tracker

2025

H.R. 884 is signed into law, repealing D.C.'s locally-passed law letting green-card-holding residents vote in local elections.

Source: Anti-DC Bill Tracker

2023

Congress overturns D.C.'s revised criminal code — 16 years in the making — only the second full block of a D.C. law in 50 years, and the first time the Senate voted to do so.

Source: Anti-DC Bill Tracker
