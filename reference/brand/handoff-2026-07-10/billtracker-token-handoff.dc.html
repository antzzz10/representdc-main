Confirmed semantics (read from the repo, not guessed)

Pulled from DEVELOPMENT-GUIDELINES.md, .claude/CLAUDE.md, and the actual component CSS — this is what each existing color currently encodes.

SignalWhat it actually means
Priority: highOn FreeDC's active opposition list, had a hearing/markup/floor vote, 20+ cosponsors, or manual override — genuine, active threat.
Priority: medium5–19 cosponsors, in committee, no hearing yet — active but not critical.
Priority: low / watchingRecently introduced, no activity — dormant.
Stage: passed-house / passed-senate / passed-bothProcedural progress through Congress — independent of priority. A bill can be low-priority and still passed-house.
Stage: enactedSigned into law — the gravest, most final state.
Floor-vote badgeA separate, time-sensitive "act now" flag — not tied to priority tier or stage.
Deep-linked highlightWayfinding only — "this is the bill you followed a link to." Not a severity signal.
Disputed by DCDC government has publicly contested this bill's legal basis or framing — a caveat, not a severity level.
Sponsor party badge (R/D/I)Neutral data, per the system's non-partisan voice rule — not a value judgment.

Decisions on the open questions

1Color remapping accuracy

Resolved by reading DEVELOPMENT-GUIDELINES.md, .claude/CLAUDE.md, and the component CSS directly rather than guessing — see the confirmed semantics and remap table above. Every existing color had a 1:1 home in tokens the system already defined; nothing needed inventing except the two items below.

2No prototype exists for dc-statehood-pledge

Sequencing puts billtracker first. When pledge comes up, worth a lightweight prototype here first (status badges, party/response filters, the respond flow) since none of its 3 routes have been designed yet — cheaper to settle layout before code than to iterate in React.

3Token drift risk

This design system stays the single source; the new green + the "no new neutral" decision are recorded here so both repos' copies stay in sync even without a shared package.

4Sequencing

Billtracker first (flagship, highest traffic), pledge site second.

Conflicts to hold in mind while implementing

Persuasion architecture doesn't apply here. STRATEGY.md's Hook→Validate→Connect→Pattern→Solution flow is for the main site's landing page. The tracker is a data/utility tool — keep the voice rules (sentence case, no emoji, "D.C." with periods, non-partisan) but don't retrofit loss-framing copy into bill cards.
DEVELOPMENT-GUIDELINES.md's sourcing policy only lives in representdc-main today. The tracker's bill data is where accurate sourcing matters most — worth copying or referencing that guardrail doc into dc-bills-tracker rather than leaving it implicit.
Legacy palette confirmed compatible. dc-bills-tracker's current colors (#DC143C, #FF8C00, bootstrap grays) are the same pre-redesign family as the old main site — the navy-workhorse / red-rationed shift applies cleanly, it's just a volume of CSS to touch, not a conflict.
Uncommitted work stays untouched. dc-bills-tracker has unstaged changes to package.json/package-lock.json/scripts/weekly-digest.js and an untracked CLAUDE.md; dc-statehood-pledge has two untracked draft .md files. None are design-related — leave them alone and keep design commits separate.
