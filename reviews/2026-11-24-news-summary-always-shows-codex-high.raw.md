1. **MATERIAL — Undated headlines can produce a false caveat** ([newsSummary.js:18](src/utils/newsSummary.js:18), [NewsAndAnalysis.jsx:177](src/NewsAndAnalysis.jsx:177)). The caveat treats the newest *parseable date* as proof that no headline arrived later. A displayed headline with a missing or invalid date could be newer, so the page can assert something it cannot establish. `validNewsItems` permits missing and null dates. In addition, `new Date(null)` parses as January 1, 1970: if that is the only headline, the summary shows with a 1969 Washington-date caveat, despite the requirement to hide it when no headline has a valid date. Add cases for null, missing and invalid dates, including a mix of dated and undated headlines.

2. **MATERIAL — A future scan timestamp can make the caveat claim a check that has not happened** ([newsSummary.js:33](src/utils/newsSummary.js:33)). `isFresh` accepts any future `lastUpdated`, even though the page rejects a timestamp more than 15 minutes ahead for “Last checked.” With an old headline and a timestamp far in the future, the summary and “No new…” caveat remain visible, potentially long after the real scan stopped. This freshness behavior predates the refactor, but the new caveat turns it into an unsupported public claim. Test future timestamps both within and beyond the allowed clock skew.

3. **MATERIAL — Old summary prose can contradict the new caveat** ([NewsAndAnalysis.jsx:177](src/NewsAndAnalysis.jsx:177)). The removed 14-day cutoff explicitly guarded against a freshly regenerated digest describing weeks-old coverage as recent. The change now displays that same summary unchanged, immediately below the caveat. For example, “No new statehood headlines since November 4” can be followed by “This week…” about those old stories. The supplied copy does not establish whether the generation pipeline prevents this; the tests use only neutral placeholder prose. Verify the pipeline’s treatment of time-sensitive language and cover an old-coverage example before shipping.

4. **NOTE — Summary validation changed beyond the age rule** ([newsSummary.js:47](src/utils/newsSummary.js:47)). Previously any truthy summary passed the visibility check; now whitespace-only strings and truthy non-string values are hidden. Hiding malformed content may be desirable, and the new tests explicitly assert it, but it is a behavior change beyond removing the headline-age cutoff. Confirm that this stricter validation is intended.

5. **NOTE — Tests do not verify the rendered placement or fallback** ([news-summary.test.mjs:59](scripts/eval/news-summary.test.mjs:59), [NewsAndAnalysis.jsx:174](src/NewsAndAnalysis.jsx:174)). The tests verify helper return values, but not that the caveat appears once inside the summary, that “Newest coverage” appears on the checked line when the summary is hidden, or that neither appears before seven days. A rendering regression could pass the suite.
