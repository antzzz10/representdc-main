**DO NOT SHIP.** The kept-summary path can restore a summary after the owner has withheld it. The new alert can also report recovery while the summary is still missing.

- **BLOCKER — A kept summary can overwrite a concurrent withholding.** [summary-memory.mjs](scripts/news/summary-memory.mjs:63), [archive.mjs](scripts/news/archive.mjs:420). Withhold stamps its time when it starts, before its push. If it starts, then a scan starts and reads the still-present summary, then the withholding pushes before the scan does, the scan keeps the old summary. At push time, `withheldAt` is earlier than `startedAt`, so `composePublication` republishes it over the owner’s removal. The fresh feed must be checked against the kept summary’s writing time on every push attempt, including retries.

- **MATERIAL — The repeated-withholding incident can close without a summary returning.** [news-incident.sh](.github/scripts/news-incident.sh:113). After two flagged scans open the incident, a `published-empty` or `summary-disabled` run takes the healthy branch and closes it with a recovery email, although no summary published. A subsequent single flagged scan can also close it if its count is below the threshold. Recovery for this incident needs evidence that a summary actually published.

- **MATERIAL — The streak is neither reliably consecutive nor based on the publication that won.** [summary-memory.mjs](scripts/news/summary-memory.mjs:101), [fetch-news.mjs](scripts/fetch-news.mjs:459). A flagged scan, a no-articles or API-failure scan, and another flagged scan produce a count of two despite the intervening scan. Separately, the count is calculated from the feed read at run start and reset when a summary is *selected*, before `composePublication` may drop it because the archive changed or a withholding won. Concurrent writers can therefore leave the feed and alert output with a stale or incorrectly reset count. Calculate and report the count from the fresh state and actual publication outcome.

- **NOTE — Retry feedback remains an instruction-injection surface.** [summarize.mjs](scripts/news/summarize.mjs:116). Removing control characters and double quotes does not neutralize instructions: a rejected draft or judge explanation can contain an apostrophe followed by “ignore the rules,” and the test itself confirms that text survives. The second judge remains a publication gate, but the retry prompt can still be steered or corrupted. The test establishes formatting and length bounds, not resistance to prompt injection.
