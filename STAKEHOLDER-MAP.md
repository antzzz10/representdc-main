# Statehood Stakeholder Map (working doc)

**Status: draft, unverified, not live anywhere.** This is a running content/planning
doc for the "meet the movement" stakeholder map — it exists to iterate on quickly, not
to ship as-is. Every org description below is seeded from the Action & Persona IA
Brainstorm doc (`reference/brand/RepresentDC Action & Persona IA Brainstorm.dc.html`)
plus general knowledge, at the same confidence level as an early outline — **none of it
has been individually fact-checked against the org's own site**, which
`DEVELOPMENT-GUIDELINES.md` requires before anything here becomes real page copy. Verify
each entry (current URL, mission-statement accuracy, still-active status) before it
ships.

## End vision

Two connected things, per the original brainstorm and confirmed 2026-07-26:

1. **A visual map** of the main orgs doing D.C. statehood work and how they relate to
   each other — not a flat logo wall, since it's a real layered coalition (a small set
   of core groups, sitting inside a much wider 100+ org coalition), and rendering that
   as one undifferentiated list would misrepresent the structure.
2. **A matching tool**: "if you want *this*, here's who to connect with" — lets a
   visitor go from their own goal (research, local organizing, party politics, student
   involvement, holding candidates accountable) straight to the org that's actually the
   right fit, instead of making them guess from a list of names they don't recognize.

The categorization below is built to serve both at once — grouped by what an org
actually *is* (for the visual map), each tagged with a plain-language "best for" line
(for the matching tool).

## Tier 2 — the wider coalition

Per the brainstorm's own recommendation: **don't reproduce the full list** — Together
for DC's steering committee alone already runs to 16 organizations (Center for Economic
Democracy, DC Appleseed, DC Fiscal Policy Institute, DC Jobs With Justice, DC Justice
Lab, DC Chapter NOW, Fair Budget Coalition, Miriam's Kitchen, National LGBTQ Task Force,
Public Citizen, SPACEs in Action, Taskforce for Democracy, and others, alongside several
Tier 1 orgs above), and a static reproduction will drift out of date. Instead: a single
line — "and a growing coalition of 100+ organizations" — crediting and linking directly
to Together for DC's own coalition page as the living source of truth, dated to whenever
we last checked it.
